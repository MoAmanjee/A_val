Questions, answered plainly.

What exactly does M³ Technologies offer?

Customised websites, AI-powered chatbots, and business automation. A project can be one of these or a combination. The scope is agreed before anything is built.

Can you customise the website for my industry?

Yes. Restaurant-related work is where most of the experience sits. The same approach can be adapted for salons, retail, professional services, and other businesses. What is included depends on your requirements and the agreed scope.

What can the AI chatbot do?

It answers from the information you provide: common questions, products or services, and hours, and it can guide someone toward a booking, an enquiry, or a purchase. It should hand over when the answer is not in that information. The preview on this site is scripted. A live assistant is a separate build.

Can the chatbot answer customers after business hours?

It can reply to questions it has been given, including when staff are off. Anything it has not been set up to handle waits for a person. After-hours behaviour is part of the scope. Some channels also limit which messages can be sent outside an open conversation.

Can you add booking or ordering functionality?

Yes, when the project needs it. Booking and ordering follow that business’s services, hours, and process. They are included when they are in the agreed scope, not by default.

Can you integrate with my existing systems?

Where a system has a supported interface, and the project includes that work, we can connect it. Each integration is confirmed during scoping. A tool you already use is not assumed to be connected.

How much does a customised website cost?

There is no single price. The quote follows the design, the pages, the functionality, any integrations, and whether you want support after launch. Send the form and we will price the scope you describe.

How long does development take?

It depends on the scope. A small site and a build with ordering, booking, or integrations take different amounts of work. A timeline is given with the quote, after we understand the business.

Can you maintain my website after launch?

Yes. Maintenance and support can be agreed separately. The fee and what is covered are confirmed for that agreement. They are not included unless the quote says so.
