The man, the bike

Some people collect stamps. Ahmed collects kilometres.

It started with one borrowed bike and a loop around the neighbourhood. These days it's clipped-in Saturday mornings, a pocket full of dates for the long climbs, and a quiet satisfaction in a perfectly clean drivetrain.

He'll tell you cycling is just exercise. It isn't. It's thinking time, it's the people he rides with, and it's the one hour of the day that belongs entirely to him.

House rules

The things Ahmed rides by

  1. 01

    Check the tyres the night before. Every single time.

  2. 02

    Nobody gets dropped on the way out. The way back is fair game.

  3. 03

    Rain is just weather. Ice is a reason to stay in.

  4. 04

    Wave at other cyclists. It costs nothing.

  5. 05

    Clean the chain before you put the bike away, not after.

  6. 06

    The ride isn't finished until the coffee is.
