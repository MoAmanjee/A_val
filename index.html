The agent stops where a person should start.

  1. Prices are yours

    Menu, shelf, and service prices are read from your data. The chat does not invent a figure to keep talking.

  2. Sending is a decision

    A quote stays a draft until the counter approves it. A kitchen ticket waits for a clear yes. Spending waits for the owner.

  3. Silence is a handover

    If the menu, the fitment list, or the diary has no answer, the chat goes to staff. So does a customer who asks for a person.

  4. Money lands with the shop

    PayFast on the restaurant build is the shop’s own merchant account. Keys stay encrypted. The public setup page never shows them back.

Your counter, then the chat, then your number.

  1. What you already sell

    The menu board, the parts list, or the appointment book.

  2. Questions staff repeat

    Flavour, vehicle, or which hour is free. Including ja, yebo, and sharp.

  3. A screen for the team

    Kitchen tickets, a quote to approve, or the day’s bookings.

    Meta’s Cloud API. A retry does not place the order twice.
