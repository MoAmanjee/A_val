Privacy

Privacy notice

This notice explains how M³ Technologies handles personal information collected on this website. It is written for people in South Africa and is meant to meet the spirit of the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA). It is not a substitute for legal advice.

Who is responsible

The responsible party is M³ Technologies. Contact details that have been published are below. A company registration number and a street address are shown only when they have been added to the site configuration. They are not invented here.

A public email address and street address have not been added to this notice yet. Until they are, use the quote form on the home page.

What we collect

If you send the quote form, we collect the name, business name, email address, WhatsApp or contact number, industry, requested service, and the description you write. We also record that you ticked the consent box. A hidden field exists to catch automated submissions. Please leave it empty.

The scripted product demonstration on the home page runs in your browser. Questions you type there are not sent to M³ Technologies and are not stored by this site.

Why we collect it

We use the enquiry to reply about a demo, a quote, or the service you asked about, and to prepare that quote. The lawful basis we rely on is your consent, given by the checkbox on the form. If you do not tick it, the form does not treat the enquiry as ready to send.

Where an enquiry goes

This website is static. It does not keep a database of enquiries.

  • If an enquiry inbox is configured, the form sends the details to that address over HTTPS. The inbox is only treated as having received them when it accepts the message.
  • If WhatsApp is configured instead, the form opens WhatsApp with the enquiry written out. You still send the message yourself. WhatsApp is operated by Meta, which may process the message outside South Africa under its own terms.
  • If neither is configured, the form tells you the enquiry was not sent. Nothing is stored.

We do not sell personal information. We do not pass an enquiry to an advertiser.

How long it is kept

An enquiry is kept long enough to respond and, if you ask us to proceed, for the quote and any agreement that follows. If you do not go ahead, it should be deleted or de-identified once it is no longer needed for that conversation. The exact period depends on the inbox that receives it.

Security

The form sends data only to an https inbox you have configured, or into a WhatsApp draft on your device. Do not include passwords, card numbers, or other secrets in the description field. No payment is taken on this website.

Cookies and analytics

The site works without non-essential cookies. Visit measurement is off unless it has been configured and you choose Allow on the banner. Refusal is stored in this browser so the banner does not return. The scripted demo does not set a tracking cookie.

If measurement is allowed, Plausible or Google Analytics 4 may receive a page view, depending on which one was configured. Google may process that information outside South Africa. You can refuse, and you can clear this site’s local storage to reset the choice.

Your rights

Under POPIA you may ask for access to the personal information we hold about you, ask for a correction, ask for deletion, or object to further processing of an enquiry. You may also withdraw the consent you gave on the form. Withdrawal does not undo a message already delivered to WhatsApp or an inbox.

Send a privacy request to the contact details above. If those details are not published yet, use the quote form and start the description with “Privacy request”.

You may lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa at inforegulator.org.za.

Children

This website is aimed at business owners. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes

If this notice changes, the updated version will be published on this page. Enquiries already sent stay under the notice that applied at the time, unless a later law requires otherwise.
