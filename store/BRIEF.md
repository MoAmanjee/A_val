# Digital products store: 90-day test brief

## Goal
Find out whether anyone buys. Hard cap on total spend: R1,500 over 90 days. Stop at day 90 if there are zero sales.
No ad spend without owner approval in the approval queue.

## Niche (one buyer, one outcome)
**Spreadsheet tools for Etsy sellers**: people who run an Etsy shop and want to know their real profit after fees.
Why this niche:
- Buyers are running businesses and will pay $9-$30 for a tool that saves time.
- Tool-based products (formulas, dashboards) sell for more than PDFs and are harder to copy casually.
- I can verify the fee maths against Etsy's published fee pages, so the products are accurate.
- Repeat buyers: someone who buys the calculator often buys the dashboard.
- Country-specific fees give many legitimate long-tail listings without making duplicate products.

Caveat: the demand figures come from seller-blog and keyword-tool sources, not audited data. Before building
all 20 listings, check each search term (for example "etsy fee calculator") on a keyword tool free tier.
Drop any term that has heavy competition and weak demand.

## Products (build in this order)
1. **Etsy Profit & Fee Calculator** (Google Sheets / Excel). Inputs: price, shipping, materials, offsite ads yes/no, seller country.
   Outputs: all Etsy fees, profit per sale, margin, break-even price. Fees per Etsy's published rates (listing $0.20, transaction 6.5%,
   payment processing by country, currency conversion 2.5%). Ship country versions: US, UK, Canada, Australia, EU, South Africa.
2. **Etsy Shop Monthly Revenue & Expense Dashboard**. Monthly sales, fees, costs, profit, charts.
3. **Product Pricing & Margin Calculator**. Works backwards from a target profit to the listing price.
4. **Etsy Listing & Keyword Tracker**. Listings, tags, views, favourites, sales, with a column for what was changed and when.
5. **Supplies & Inventory Tracker** for handmade sellers (reorder levels, cost per unit).
6. **Bundle**: "Complete Etsy Seller Toolkit" (products 1-5), priced above any single product.

## Listing plan (target 20 listings by end of week 2)
- 6 country versions of product 1, 1 each of products 2-5, 1 bundle = 11 listings.
- 9 more from keyword research: variations aimed at specific seller types (digital sellers, print-on-demand sellers, jewellery
  makers, candle makers, etc.), each with a genuinely different input set, not a copy.

## Listing requirements
- 5-10 images per listing (mockups, screenshot walkthrough, "what you get").
- Title: lead with the exact buyer search phrase. All 13 tags used. Long description with what is included and how to open the file.
- Delivery: .xlsx file plus a PDF with a "make a copy" Google Sheets link (only if a Google account is available).
- Price: $9-$19 for single tools, $29-$39 for the bundle.

## Etsy policy
- Items must be listed as **Designed by a seller**. If AI was used to produce any part (images, copy, structure), state it in the
  listing description. Selling AI prompts alone is not allowed.
- The seller account holder must be a real person who owns the shop.

## Roles
- **Agent**: research, build the files, write all listing copy, create mockup images, track results, suggest improvements.
- **Owner**: create and verify the Etsy account, link the bank account, upload listings (copy-paste from the prepared files),
  approve any spend.

## Decision rule at day 90
- 0 sales: stop. Total loss is capped at R1,500.
- 1-9 sales: keep going 30 more days, improve the listings that got views but no sales.
- 10+ sales: add products in the best-selling sub-niche and consider a small approved ad test.
